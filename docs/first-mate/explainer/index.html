Read the narration

Long work needs more than a long conversation. The companion service keeps the work log, tracks execution, and watches for trouble. When a session needs a fresh start, its checkpoint preserves the assignment, decisions, evidence, and next action. A successor loads that checkpoint and verifies its workspace before taking over. The old session becomes history, not a loose end. You can follow what happened in the feature journal. First Mate stays focused on the feature, while the system keeps track of the details.

You shape the route.

Each major stage ends with a human checkpoint. The smaller coding, testing, and review loops can work autonomously within that stage, while explicit internal human gates remain in place.

  1. “Start by exploring the idea.”

    First Mate assigns focused investigation and planning work.

  2. “The plan looks good. Implement it.”

    Your direction authorizes implementation. The work begins asynchronously.

  3. “Run the reviews, then show me the evidence.”

    Independent reviewers inspect the work and return their findings.

  4. “Change this part before we continue.”

    First Mate records the revised direction and updates the affected work.

No reply means the feature waits.Finishing a stage never silently authorizes the next one. Cleanup that deletes branches or worktrees is a separate decision.
