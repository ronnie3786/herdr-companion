First Mate

First Mate HUD

Design reference for the chosen First Mate HUD. Collapsed, a row of halo orbs sits under First Mate; expanded, the features hang on one lit line as rows. The features are made up, and nothing here connects to Herdr.

How many features

Anything that needs you gets its own orb and row first. The row shows six orbs at most, the last becoming "+N". The list shows three moving rows plus a summary row you can open.

Try it
Gestures
  • Click First Mate to type. Press and hold it to talk, and let go to send. Dragging it still moves it.
  • Hover a circle or row for details. Click to open its session.
  • A dashed lavender ring or a bubble means a message is waiting. Click it to read and reply in place.
  • Drag a feature onto First Mate to ask about it.
  • Drag First Mate. Everything follows.
  • Right-click a feature, or First Mate, to change its emoji and label.
  • Esc closes things and cancels listening.
Reading it
    Motion
    How it could work
    • One lead. First Mate becomes a new agent above each feature's mate. It reads their boards and passes your words to the right one. It can't approve a stage on its own; your decisions go through as your messages.
    • A steady order. Anything that needs you sits on top. Everything else keeps the order it started in, so a row only moves when its status changes.
    • Overflow never hides what needs you. Collapsed, at most six orbs show, and the sixth becomes a "+N" orb when there are more. Expanded, three moving rows show, then a summary row. Opening it makes the moving rows compact, so fourteen features still fit.
    • A glance when collapsed. One circle per feature keeps its emoji and status color, and a ring shows a waiting message, so you can tell who needs you without opening the list.
    • Small, fast data. The HUD polls one compact fleet summary (status, step, a one-line "now", unread count), never full snapshots.
    • Read state lives on the server, so the Mac, iPhone, and web agree on what's unread.
    • Voice reuses the HUD's recorder and transcription, then sends straight to First Mate.
    • Theme tokens only. HUD glass at 78%, lavender for First Mate and its signals, Herdr's pastel statuses, and First Mate's attention orange for "your turn".
    • Cheap to draw. The rows are plain views. The line, signals, and scan share one Canvas layer, and nothing moves while idle.
    • Version 1: core, list, rings, badges, readouts, speech bubbles, text and voice. Version 2: signals and scan, drag-to-ask, one-tap replies.
    Spec
    • First Mate: 86 px circle, a glass disc with a light-panel face. Click to type, press and hold 0.4 s to talk, drag to move. A count badge shows how many features need you.
    • Collapsed: one row of halo orbs 34 px apart, 30 px each, no backing, 23 px under First Mate. Six orbs at most; the sixth becomes "+N" with one ring segment per tucked feature. A chevron under the row opens the list.
    • Halo orb: dark glass core with the emoji, and the six-step progress ring in its status color with a soft glow. A dashed lavender ring turning slowly means a message is waiting.
    • Expanded: rows on one lit line, 38 px tall, 46 px apart, 236 px glass slats. Needs-you rows first, a small diamond, then moving rows in start order. Three moving rows, then a summary row; opening it turns moving rows into 24 px compact rows.
    • Status: blocked #E2A7B6, your turn #FF9F0A, ready #9CCDB9, working #E4C386, First Mate and messages #AAA6F4. Glass at 78% on #151519.
    • Names: user labels can be up to 100 Unicode code points after trimming; the row truncates a long label with an ellipsis. Default labels still clip the feature title to 24 code points. The hover card shows the full feature title.

    Explorations: holographic list, third, second and first list versions, holographic, compact Jarvis, full-screen Jarvis and ropes.
