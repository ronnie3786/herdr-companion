# Herdr for Mac 0.6.5 beta 1

- Aligned Notes with the orb's bottom-left corner, mirroring Mic at bottom-right while keeping X at top-right.
- Added Settings → HUD → Visible agents. The default is 4; the fifth agent appears under a compact circular +1. Choose a limit from 1–20, or Show all for an uncapped, scrollable agent list. Clicking +N temporarily reveals every agent, including lists larger than 20.
- Replaced the chat HUD's heavy shadow with softer, low-opacity ambient and contact shadows.

## Try it

Hover over the orb to reveal its corner controls. In Settings → HUD, choose 5 to group starting with the sixth agent, or Show all to disable grouping. Your preference is saved across app launches. The +N circle grows only enough to fit its count and text-size preference, rather than reserving a full session row.

## Compatibility

Mac-only update; no companion server, Pi extension, or iPhone update is required. This preview uses development signing and is not notarized.
